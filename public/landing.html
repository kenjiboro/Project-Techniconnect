Once a Technologian, always connected

TechniConnect is the home for CIT-U alumni. Register once, get verified by Alumni Affairs, and find classmates and job openings.

Register as alumni Log in

What you can do here

Get verified

Upload a government-issued ID. Alumni Affairs staff check it, and you get an email when your request is reviewed.

Find fellow alumni

Search the alumni directory by name, batch, college or program. You appear only if you choose to.

See job offers

Alumni Affairs posts openings, and you see the ones open to everyone or meant for your college or program.

Stay informed

Read official announcements from the university and keep your contact details up to date.

How it works

  1. Register

    Fill in your details and upload your ID.

  2. Wait for review

    Alumni Affairs verifies your request and emails you the result.

  3. Log in and connect

    Update your profile, choose what to share, and browse the directory.

Your privacy comes first

Your details are never public. Only verified alumni who turn on directory visibility appear in the directory, and each person chooses which details other alumni can see. Full records are available only to authorized Alumni Affairs staff.
